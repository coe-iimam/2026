Explorer
You are here
No idea or team yet - places to get curious, learn the basics, and find people to build with.
What to do now
  1. Take YC Startup School - one or two hours a week is enough.
  2. Keep a list of problems you notice at work, internships, or around you.
  3. Meet potential co-founders - attend CoE events and make a YC Co-Founder Matching profile.

Ideation
You are here
Have a problem or idea, not yet tested - frameworks for shaping a problem before you build anything.
What to do now
  1. Write one line: who has this problem, and how they solve it today.
  2. Fill a Lean Canvas in under an hour - it shows what you don't know yet.
  3. Talk to 10 people who have the problem. Ask about their past behaviour, not your idea.
The Mom Test
Visit ↗
The standard reference for asking customers questions that reveal the truth, not polite encouragement.
FrameworkAlways open

Visit ↗
Ash Maurya's one-page tool for mapping a business idea before writing a single line of code.
FrameworkAlways open

Validation
You are here
Early conversations look promising - tools for testing demand properly before you build.
What to do now
  1. Decide in advance what result counts as "validated" (e.g. 30% of visitors sign up).
  2. Put up a one-page landing site and measure real sign-ups, not likes.
  3. Run 20+ customer interviews and note the pain points that keep coming up.

Visit ↗

ToolFree tier available

MVP / Build
You are here
Building your first working version - free credits and tools from the major platforms.
What to do now
  1. Build only the one feature that solves the core problem. Use no-code tools where you can.
  2. Claim free cloud credits before you start paying for anything.
  3. Set a launch date for your first users and stick to it.

4
Pilot
You are here
Getting your first real users - places to launch and collect honest feedback.
What to do now
  1. Get 10-50 real users and watch how they actually use the product.
  2. Pick one key number (e.g. weekly active users) and track it every week.
  3. Launch publicly once you can act on feedback quickly.

Funding-ready
You are here
Traction, pitch deck, and financials ready - government and private funding routes.
What to do now
  1. Get DPIIT recognition - most government schemes need it.
  2. Prepare a 10-12 slide pitch deck and a simple financial model.
  3. List grants and investors that fit your stage and sector, then apply in batches.
Startup India / DPIIT Recognition
Visit ↗
Register for DPIIT recognition first - it's the prerequisite for MAARG, tax benefits, and most government schemes below.
GovernmentOpen - apply anytime
